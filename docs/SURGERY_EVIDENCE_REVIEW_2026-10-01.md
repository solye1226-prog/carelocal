# Surgery guide evidence review

Reviewed: 2026-10-01. Scope: surgery-classification, cataract-surgery-benefit, gallbladder-surgery-benefit.

## Findings and edits

- The KNIA link is a real official page, but its scope is simplified actual-expense claim documents, not fixed surgery-benefit tiers. Corrected the labels and explained its limited scope.
- Replaced the Naver Pay general explanation in the classification source list with an insurer's actual policy example and official claim-document instructions.
- Added a contract/operation/table-entry inquiry worksheet. No universal tier or benefit amount is assigned.
- Cataract: distinguished initial lens surgery from subsequent YAG posterior capsulotomy, with a dated consumer-information case. Kept policy-specific classification separate from lens expense and bilateral payment-count questions.
- Gallbladder: added a record checklist separating cholecystectomy, bile-duct stone treatment, dates and approach. Did not infer tiers from procedure codes.
- Reworded document preparation as checking existing records against the user's insurer's requirements before purchasing additional documents.
- Added visible site-operator attribution with editorial-policy and correction links. No professional credential or expert-review claim was added.
- Titles, canonical URLs, advertising scripts and unrelated local changes were preserved.

   https://direct.samsungfire.com/m/claim/MP040202_001.html?tab=1
3. KNIA simplified actual-expense claim documentation. Supports actual-expense preparation and possibility of additional review documents; not fixed-benefit classification.
   https://consumer.knia.or.kr/m/consumer/insurance-guide/0202.do

5. December 2023 consumer notice hosted by Korea Insurance Research Institute, PDF pages 3-4. Reports a cataract lens operation versus YAG posterior capsulotomy classification case; not every policy's tiers. No tier number from the case was assigned to readers.
   https://kiri.or.kr/PDF/weeklytrend/20231226/trend20231226_2.pdf
6. KDCA gallstones information, treatment section: location, symptoms and coexisting conditions affect treatment. Supports distinction of diagnosis and actual treatment, not recommendations for the reader's treatment.
   https://health.kdca.go.kr/healthinfo/biz/health/gnrlzHealthInfo/gnrlzHealthInfo/gnrlzHealthInfoView.do?cntnts_sn=6735
