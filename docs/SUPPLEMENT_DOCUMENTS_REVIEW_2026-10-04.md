# Supplement documents reinforcement

- Scope: existing supplement-documents article; contextual inbound links in treatment-material, surgery documents, and Leqembi articles.
- Preserve titles, canonical URLs, publication dates, images and advertising settings.
- Distinguish supplemental requests from denial and approval. Explain request purpose, relevant dates, accepted alternatives, secure submission and receipt confirmation.
- Do not prescribe universal documents, payment deadlines or automatic reimbursement of issuance fees.
- Sources checked 2026-10-04:
  - https://direct.samsungfire.com/m/claim/MP040202_001.html?tab=1 : benefit-specific records and conditional additional documents; one insurer's example, not universal requirements.
  - https://www.kbinsure.co.kr/CG205020003.ec : disease claim documentation by benefit; one insurer's example.
- No indexing requests, reminders, advertising changes or analytics causation claims.
